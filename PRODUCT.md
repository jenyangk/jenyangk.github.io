# PRODUCT.md

## What this is

jenyangkoh.com — personal portfolio for Andy Koh, a full-stack software engineer.

## Audience (in priority order)

1. **Recruiters** — 6-10 second first pass. Must see: name, current title, location, seniority signal, stack.
2. **Senior engineers** (fintech/finance target) — want proof of systems work: latencies, scale, hard technical calls.
3. **Senior managers** — want judgment and autonomy: problems seen and owned without being assigned.

## Career intent

- Seeking new work outside current environment.
- Locations: Canada, Singapore.
- Industry interest: **fintech / finance** — "systems where correctness matters."

## Voice

Specific over adjectival. Show, don't narrate. The personality comes from:
- The animated kaomoji mascot (demonstrated, not declared)
- Live data (DouDou's real vote count)
- Specific numbers in experience (p95 300ms, 2M+ events/day, 200+ sites)
- Quiet, concrete personal details (badminton, cooking, astrophotography)

Not from: performed enthusiasm, self-narrating personas, sparkle emojis, "Let's build something together."

## Core design decisions

1. **One mascot, many moods.** A single animated kaomoji replaces the four agents. It blinks, eyes follow cursor, falls asleep on idle tab, changes expression by section, celebrates on email copy. `(⌐■_■)` default, moods per section.

2. **No theatrical intro.** The staged four-agent typewriter sequence is replaced with an immediate content reveal. The AsciiCanvas scaffold background remains as the ambient identity layer.

3. **Resume-first experience section.** Source of truth: `src/assets/Resume.md`. Lead with outcomes and metrics, not narrative memoir. Latium SE-II is the anchor block.

4. **Personality arrives after the work.** "Off the screen" is a single quiet line near the bottom, not a section. Badminton, cooking, astrophotography. The bookshelf has 3 books with one-line takes, astronomy leaning.

5. **Fintech signal, not a fintech costume.** One explicit line in the hero. Demonstrated through reliable-systems framing, not stock tickers or navy-and-gold.
